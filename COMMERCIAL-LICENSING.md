# Commercial Licensing of Kontrakt

**Kontrakt is source-available. Commercial use is not covered by the free license.**

The [Kontrakt Noncommercial Source License 1.0](../../Downloads/Kontrakt_License_1.0_Actual_Repository_Patch_20261103/LICENSE)
allows personal noncommercial use, noncommercial forks with corresponding source, and qualifying noncommercial
educational and public-interest research. It is not an OSI-approved open-source license.

## When commercial authorization is required

A separate commercial authorization is required for covered-code acts performed as part of a business or paid
professional workflow, including **internal** development, procurement evaluation, research and development,
compilation, testing, CI/CD, verification, optimization, infrastructure, maintenance and operations. This applies
regardless of industry, direct revenue, realized profit, whether the compiler is distributed, or whether a personal
device or account is used for the work.

Business authorization is required when a developer, consultant, contractor or research provider performs such work for
an identifiable commercial customer. An independent academic publication or personal project is not retroactively made
commercial merely because someone later benefits from it.

## Commercial licensing routes

- **Internal Commercial Use:** For specified legal entities, versions, users, systems, contractors and permitted
  workflows, including internal development and CI/CD.
- **OEM / Redistribution:** For distributing Kontrakt-owned compiler components, developer tools, or other protected
  code beyond an authorized internal use.
- **Hosted / Third-Party Compilation:** For operating Kontrakt as a build, compilation, verification or development
  service for other legal entities.
- **Named Authorization:** The rightsholder may grant an identified startup, company, individual or research institution
  free or discounted commercial permission for a specified period and purpose. It does not automatically extend to
  affiliates, parent companies, acquirers, customers, contractors or other parties.
- **Evaluation Authorization:** Businesses may request a written, limited assessment grant. There is no automatic
  commercial evaluation period under the public license.

A standard commercial agreement does **not** automatically include sublicensing, OEM redistribution, third-party hosted
compiler services or licenses for separate legal entities.

## Pricing and agreements

Licenses may be priced by legal entity, developer, build agent, project, deployment, or a clearly documented
combination. A price or metric is binding only if included in an agreement or order accepted by the relevant
rightsholder. Support, maintenance, warranties and service levels are separate unless expressly included.

A commercial license becomes effective **only when an authorized rightsholder grants the relevant permission in a valid
written agreement or other authenticated authorization**. This page is informational and is not itself an offer of
additional commercial rights.

### Request authorization

Contact the official Kontrakt repository maintainer through the repository's GitHub issue tracker or another verified
contact channel published by the rightsholder. Use a private channel for business-sensitive information. Include the
legal entity, intended activities, applicable versions, deployment scope and whether OEM or hosted use is requested. A
public issue, message or invoice alone is **not** a grant of commercial rights.

## Forks and contributed code

A fork author may license only rights that author actually owns or controls. Renaming or partially rewriting Kontrakt
does not supply a commercial license to protected Kontrakt-owned expression. An enterprise using a fork must clear both
upstream Kontrakt commercial rights and any separate third-party addition rights.

Official upstream contributions are accepted only subject to the project's applicable contribution-rights procedure.
This license does not assume that third-party copyright or patent rights are automatically assigned by a pull request.

## Compiler outputs and components

A normal independent application is not automatically relicensed merely because Kontrakt compiled it, and a downstream
customer does not owe a compiler fee solely for purchasing that independent application. If an output **actually embeds
copyright-protected Kontrakt runtime or helper code**, the embedded component's applicable licensing and redistribution
permissions must be checked separately.
